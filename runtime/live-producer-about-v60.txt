Five generations have been dedicated to breeding 100% Iberian pigs , a breed they have known from the very beginning and around which they have developed their entire way of working. Everything starts in the countryside. The pigs are born and raised free-range, first alongside their mothers and, once they can fend for themselves, making use of the resources they find in the dehesa. Piglets born in autumn may even enjoy their first acorns during weaning; later comes the montanera , when the animals destined for acorn finishing complete their final fattening period by feeding on acorns, grass and other natural resources from the surrounding land. The dehesa remains part of their diet throughout the rest of the year. Over the years, that experience in breeding Iberian pigs led Hidalgo de la Jara to take responsibility for making their products as well. Three generations have been dedicated to raising cattle in the Montes de Toledo, an experience passed from one generation to the next that continues to shape the way they understand livestock farming. Their farm is located on a dehesa on the northern side of the Montes de Toledo , an environment particularly well suited to the natural growth of pasture. For much of the year, the animals graze freely and make direct use of the resources provided by the land, maintaining a way of farming that is closely connected to the territory. Their diet is adapted to the conditions of each season. During the months of greatest abundance, some of the pasture is harvested and stored for winter and, when the animals' diet needs to be supplemented, they use 100% natural feed made exclusively from plant-based ingredients . This feed is produced at the Menasalbas cooperative of which they are members, a connection that allows them to follow its production closely and maintain greater control over the feeding of their cattle .
